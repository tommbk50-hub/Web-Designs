Introducing the LifeTime Lingual™ ceramic hybrid retainer.

The LifeTime Lingual™ is a completely new concept in final orthodontic retention. Unlike traditional lingual retainers, there are no stainless steel wires, metal bonding pads, or fibres to fracture or debond.

CAD-CAM designed and precision milled in high-strength ceramic hybrid (only 1.0mm thick) to hold lower 3–3 teeth securely, comfortably, and hygienically. Delivered diamond-polished and pre-etched with a custom Essix-type location jig for predictable chairside bonding in under 30 minutes.
